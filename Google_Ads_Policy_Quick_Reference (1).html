01

Classify the account first

Answer these before building anything, the answers decide which tools you're even allowed to use.

Sensitive category?

Health, finance, legal, housing, employment, addiction, weight loss, dating → triggers personalized-ads limits.

Certification needed?

Gambling, pharmacy/telehealth, addiction treatment, financial services, crypto all require Google approval before launch.

Geo-restricted?

Alcohol, gambling, political, and cannabis rules vary hard by country and even by state.

Minors in the audience?

Under-18 users are never eligible for personalized ads, in any category.

02

Policy cheat sheet

Signal pills show the practical risk level, not a legal classification.

Certification required

Healthcare & medicines

No direct-to-consumer prescription drug ads. Pharmacy/telehealth/addiction need LegitScript. No guaranteed-cure language.

Certification required

Gambling

Approved countries only, responsible-gambling info on landing page, never target minors.

Restricted

Financial services

Loans/credit repair/crypto need certification. Must disclose rates and fees, no "guaranteed approval."

Restricted

Alcohol

Approved countries only, no minors, no glorifying excessive drinking.

Restricted

Political

Advertiser identity verification in most markets; rules shift fast and vary hard by region.

Prohibited (mostly)

Dangerous products

Weapons and explosives are largely banned; narrow exceptions for safety gear.

Prohibited (mostly)

Adult content

Explicit content banned; suggestive content only in limited formats/geos.

Universal rule

Misrepresentation & editorial

Ad and landing page must match. No hidden fees, fake urgency, or impersonation. Clean spelling/grammar, fast page load.

04

Creative, do / avoid

Do

  • Qualified, evidence-based claims ("may help support")
  • Transparent pricing and fee disclosures
  • Neutral CTAs, "Learn more," "Get a quote"
  • Landing page shows business ID, contact info, privacy policy
  • Certification badges displayed where required

Avoid

  • "Guaranteed cure," "guaranteed approval," "risk-free"
  • Diagnostic phrasing ("if you have [symptom]...")
  • Manufactured urgency ("only you were selected")
  • Graphic medical imagery or shock before/after photos
  • Unauthorized trademarks or copyrighted assets

06

Platform differences

Search

Most forgiving for sensitive categories, intent-based, not identity-based.

Performance Max

One sensitive signal can restrict the whole campaign's audience signals, hard to isolate the cause.

Display

Sensitive creative + Custom Segments only serves contextually or to non-sensitive audiences.

YouTube

Same personalization limits as Display, plus content-adjacency rules.

Demand Gen

Uses curated audiences by default, may be fully blocked for sensitive categories.

Shopping

Separate product-level policy layer; feed can be disapproved independent of ad copy.
